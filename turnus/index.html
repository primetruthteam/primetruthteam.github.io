Turnus

A shift calendar that knows your week.

Turnus is a shift planner for iPhone. Tap a day and pick a shift, or set your rotation once and see the whole year. Your next shift sits on the lock screen, your hours add up as you go, and you can swap a shift with a colleague in a couple of taps.

Turnus has no server. There is no account, no analytics and no ads, so there is no company holding your roster. Your shifts live on your iPhone and, if you turn it on, in your own private iCloud.

Privacy Policy — Turnus

Turnus stores your shifts, shift types, workplaces, pay rules and leave allowance on your iPhone and, if iCloud is on, in your own iCloud account (CloudKit private database). Teams you create are shared through CloudKit sharing with the people you invite; only members of that share can see the team calendar. We run no server and receive no data.

Turnus reads your iOS calendar only when you turn on "Show iOS Calendar events", and writes to it only when you tap "Add shifts to iOS Calendar". Notifications are scheduled on your device. Purchases go through the App Store; we never see payment details. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.

EU users: the legal basis is performance of the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); data stays in your Apple account and is not transferred to us; you can delete everything from Settings › Delete all data. Contact: zenDesign219@gmail.com.

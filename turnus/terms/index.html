1. What Turnus is

A shift calendar and a pay estimator. It is not a payroll system, a timekeeping system of record, or a legal, tax or financial service, and it gives no advice of any kind.

2. What the pay figure is — and is not

Turnus multiplies the hours you entered by the rate and premiums you entered. It is an estimate before tax and deductions, and it knows nothing about your contract, your collective agreement or your country's overtime law. Your employer's payslip is the number that counts. Never use a Turnus figure to dispute pay without checking it yourself.

3. Your content

Your shifts, workplaces, rules and notes are yours. Turnus claims no rights to them, never transmits them to us, and lets you export them to CSV or PDF at any time, free.

4. Teams and swaps

A team is a share you create. Inviting someone gives them your shift times for that team calendar, and accepting a swap changes both calendars. Only invite people you work with: an invite link is a key, and anyone who opens it joins. The team owner can remove members, and any member can leave. There is no moderation and no dispute process — a swap is an agreement between you and a colleague, not a contract with us.

5. Backups are your responsibility

Turnus stores shifts on your device and, when enabled, in your private iCloud. If iCloud is off, a lost or wiped phone means lost shifts. The Settings screen says so when backup is off. Use Export to keep your own copy.

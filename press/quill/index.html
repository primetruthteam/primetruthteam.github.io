Quill — press kit

One-liner

Quill is a diary for iPhone that locks with Face ID and never asks for a subscription.

Short (≤ 280)

A diary that stays yours. Quill keeps what you write on your iPhone — no account, no server, nothing for anyone else to read. Face ID or PIN lock, full-text search, import from Day One and Apple Journal, export to PDF or Markdown. One-time purchase for Pro, no subscription.

Long (1 đoạn)

Quill is a private diary for iPhone built on one architectural promise: there is no server, so there is no company that could read your entries — including the one that made it. It locks with a four-digit PIN and Face ID, covers its preview in the App Switcher, and lets you back in with a recovery code if you forget the PIN. Writing is plain and quick: bold, italic, bullets, up to twenty photos an entry, a mood in words, tags, and a draft that saves as you type. Full-text search runs on the device in milliseconds; a month calendar shows the days you wrote; On this day brings back the same date from earlier years. Old diaries come over from Day One, Apple Journal, Markdown, plain text or CSV — with a preview before anything is written and no duplicates on a second import. Everything exports again to PDF, to a book with a cover and contents, or to Markdown with photos. Backup goes through your own private iCloud database, not ours. Quill Pro — more photos, reading fonts, more notebooks, a widget, advanced filters, place and weather — is a single one-time purchase; the lock, backup, import, export and search are free and always will be. English and Vietnamese.

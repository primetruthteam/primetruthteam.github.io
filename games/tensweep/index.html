Swipe, add up to 10, and sweep the board clean!

TenSweep is a fast, friendly number puzzle built around one simple rule: swipe across neighboring tiles that add up to exactly 10 to clear them. Easy to learn in seconds, hard to put down. No account, no sign-up, and no in-app purchases — the whole game is yours from the first swipe.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Swipe across two or more adjacent tiles whose numbers add up to exactly 10: 6+4, 2+3+5, 1+2+3+4…
  • Cleared tiles vanish and new ones drop in, so the board keeps flowing
  • Longer chains score more, and back-to-back clears build a combo multiplier
  • Reach a x5 combo to set off a MEGA COMBO celebration
  • Out of moves? The board reshuffles itself, so you can never get stuck

JOURNEY: 240 LEVELS, 12 CHAPTERS

Ride along with a little delivery truck hauling numbered crates from the Neighborhood through Downtown, Harbor, Airport, Highway, Mountain Pass, Night Town, Train Depot and beyond, all the way to the Final Stop. Every level gives you a limited number of moves and its own goal: reach a target score, clear a set number of a specific digit, or build long chains. Finish with moves to spare to earn up to 3 stars. Boards start at a cozy 5×5 grid and grow to 8×8 as the route gets tougher.

MORE WAYS TO PLAY

  • Time Attack: 75 seconds to score as high as you can
  • Zen: no timer, no move limit, play until you feel like stopping
  • Daily Challenge: the same board for every player, once a day. Can you top the leaderboard?

COMPETE & SHARE

  • Game Center leaderboards for High Score, Daily Challenge and Total Stars
  • 13 achievements to unlock as you complete chapters and land big combos
  • Share your score or level result with one tap
  • 240 levels with a carefully tuned difficulty curve
  • Interactive tutorial that teaches the game in under a minute
  • Colorblind-safe mode with distinct tile patterns and borders
  • Music, sound effects and vibration you can toggle anytime
  • Play in English or Vietnamese, switch instantly in Settings
  • Progress is saved right on your device, no internet needed to play
  • Quick sessions: a Time Attack run takes just over a minute

SAFE FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY

  • No account or personal information required to play, ever
  • Ads are always non-personalized, and we never ask for tracking permission
  • Optional reward ads for a few extra moves or seconds, never required to progress
  • Gentle gameplay with no violence, no chat and nothing scary

Whether you've got 2 minutes or 20, TenSweep is the perfect brain-tickling break. Swipe, sum, sweep, and see how far the truck can go.

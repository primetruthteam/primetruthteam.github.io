Welcome to Bloom Sort! Pour, sort, and decorate your way through 12 cozy chapters — 180 levels, Daily Challenges, and a room to call your own.

Pour, sort, and unwind! Bloom Sort is a cozy color-sorting puzzle where every level you solve helps decorate a brand new room. Simple to learn, endlessly satisfying, and completely free to play.

HOW TO PLAY

Tap a tube to pick it up, then tap another to pour. Match same-colored liquid together until every tube holds just one color. That's it — pure, relaxing puzzle logic with a satisfying pour animation on every move.

DECORATE AS YOU GO

Every level you win unlocks a new piece of furniture for that chapter's room. Clear all 15 levels in a chapter and watch an empty space become a fully decorated little world — then move on to the next themed room.

  • 180 handcrafted levels across 12 beautifully themed chapters
  • A real pour animation — watch liquid slosh and flow between tubes
  • Unlock and decorate a unique room for every chapter
  • Daily Challenge with its own streak to keep
  • Earn coins and up to 3 stars per level
  • Undo and Hint to help you out of a tricky spot
  • No sign-up, no internet required to play — your progress is saved right on your device
  • Relaxing pastel art style, easy on the eyes for a quick break anytime

Bloom Sort is designed for everyone, including kids — every ad shown is non-personalized, and we never ask for tracking permission.

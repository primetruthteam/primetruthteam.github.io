Stuck in traffic? You're the one who gets to fix it.

Traffic Escape: Car Puzzle is a relaxing, family-friendly sliding puzzle — completely free to play, with no account, no sign-up, and nothing to buy to enjoy the full game.

Every level is a tightly packed grid of cars, trucks, and buses. Slide them out of each other's way, one smart move at a time, until every vehicle escapes the board. No timers, no pressure — just you, the grid, and the satisfaction of a perfectly cleared jam.

SAFE FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY

  • No account or personal information required to play, ever
  • Ads shown are always non-personalized — we don't track you or your kids
  • Simple, gentle gameplay with no violence, no chat, and nothing scary
  • Your progress is saved privately, right on your own device

HOW TO PLAY

  • Drag any vehicle forward or backward along its lane
  • Vehicles can only move in a straight line — plan your order carefully
  • Clear the board in as few moves as possible to earn up to 3 stars

410 FREE LEVELS, 12 CITIES

Drive through Downtown, Harbor Side, Neon Night, Desert Highway, Mountain Pass, and Sky Metro... — each with its own look and rising difficulty, from simple warmups to satisfying multi-step challenges. All 410 levels are unlocked through play — nothing is ever paywalled.

STUCK? NO PROBLEM

  • Undo your last move if you change your mind
  • Use a free Hint when you're really stuck

COLLECT & CUSTOMIZE

Earn coins just by playing, then spend them on new car liveries — including special Ambulance, Police, and Taxi skins.

COME BACK DAILY

Come back each day for a free, growing coin streak.

Whether you've got 2 minutes or 20, Traffic Escape: Car Puzzle is the perfect free, family-safe puzzle for clearing your head — and clearing the road.

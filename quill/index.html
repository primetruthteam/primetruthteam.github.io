Quill

A notebook only you can read.

Quill is a private diary for iPhone. Write in thirty seconds, lock it with a PIN or Face ID, find any day in two taps, and take everything out whenever you like — free.

Quill has no server. There is no account, no analytics and no ads, so there is no company that could read your diary. Your entries live on your iPhone and, if you turn it on, in your own private iCloud.

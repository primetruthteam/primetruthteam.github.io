Common questions

I forgot my PIN. Tap "Forgot your PIN?" on the lock screen and type the recovery code you saved. If you lost that too, delete Quill and install it again: the lock is removed, and your entries come back from iCloud if backup was on.

My entries are not on my new iPhone. Check both phones are signed in to the same Apple ID and that iCloud backup shows "On" in Quill's Settings. Sync can take a few minutes on first launch.

Import found 0 entries. For Day One, choose the .zip from Settings › Import/Export › Export JSON, or the unzipped folder. For Apple Journal, choose the exported folder. The preview shows what was read before anything is written.

Where are my exports? The share sheet lets you save the PDF or folder to Files, AirDrop it, or send it to another app.

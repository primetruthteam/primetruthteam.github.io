1. The short version

ScrollPause collects nothing. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Everything the app records stays on your iPhone. Nothing about which apps you pause, or how often you pause them, is ever transmitted anywhere — not to us, and not to anyone else.

The rest of this page explains exactly what is stored, where, and how to remove it.

2. Who we are

ScrollPause is made by PrimeTruth Team, an independent developer. For any question about this policy or about your data, write to zenDesign219@gmail.com. We answer in English or Vietnamese.

3. Data we collect about you

None. ScrollPause has no user accounts, no sign-in, no server of its own, and no analytics or advertising software of any kind. We do not receive your name, email address, device identifier, location, contacts, photos, or usage figures. We could not identify a single user of ScrollPause if we tried.

Because we collect no data, ScrollPause does not track you across apps or websites, does not use the App Tracking Transparency prompt, and does not share, sell, or disclose personal information to anyone.

About Apple's Screen Time data

ScrollPause uses Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity frameworks. When you choose apps to pause, iOS hands the app an opaque token for each one — a value that identifies the app to iOS but that we cannot read, decode, or turn back into an app name. Those tokens are stored on your device only.

Apple requires that Screen Time information never leave the device on which it was collected. ScrollPause complies with that requirement absolutely: it has no network code for this purpose and no server to send anything to.

The recovery-code file

When you tap Share the code on the recovery-code screen, the app writes a small text file into its own temporary folder so that the iOS share sheet can hand it to AirDrop, Mail, or another app you choose. That file is protected with complete file protection, is never readable by other apps, and is deleted as soon as you leave the screen.

5. Notifications

If you turn on the weekly reminder, ScrollPause schedules a local notification on your device for Sunday evening. There is no push server involved and no message is sent over the internet. iOS will ask your permission the first time; if you decline, the switch turns itself off and nothing is scheduled.

6. Purchases

ScrollPause Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system using StoreKit. Apple processes the payment, not us. We never see your payment card, your billing address, or your Apple Account details. The only thing the app learns from Apple is whether a valid purchase exists, which it uses to unlock Pro features. We keep no receipt of our own and no purchase history on any server.

7. Third parties

ScrollPause contains no third-party software development kits — no advertising network, no crash reporter, no analytics provider, no social-media library. The only external code in the app is Apple's own frameworks, which run on your device.

Because nothing is collected and nothing is transmitted, there is no sharing with third parties, no sale of personal information, and no international transfer of data to disclose.

8. Children

ScrollPause is rated 4+ and is intended for general audiences. It is not directed to children under 13 and is not distributed in the App Store Kids Category. Since the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

9. Your rights and how to exercise them

Privacy laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, and Vietnam's Decree 13/2023/ND-CP give you rights over personal data held about you — to see it, correct it, delete it, or take it elsewhere. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, or transfer.

Over the data on your own device you have complete control at all times:

  • Delete everything inside the app: Settings › Delete all data. This removes every pause record, every quiet-hours schedule, and every app you had chosen, and lifts the shields.
  • Delete the app: removing ScrollPause from your iPhone deletes its database, its App Group container, and its app-lock record. Nothing of it remains, and nothing was ever stored elsewhere.
  • Withdraw Screen Time permission: iPhone Settings › Screen Time. ScrollPause will notice and tell you that nothing is being paused.

If you believe your privacy rights have been infringed, you may contact us at the address above, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

The one thing we do process. If you write to us, your email address and whatever you put in the message reach us — that is the only personal data we ever hold. We use it to answer you and keep it only as long as that takes. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in replying to the people who write to us (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Complaints. If you believe we have handled your data badly, you may lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of the EU or EEA country where you live or work, or with the Information Commissioner's Office in the United Kingdom.

International transfers. We transfer nothing, because we receive nothing. An email you send us arrives in a mailbox we hold in Vietnam. Everything else — the App Store, Screen Time, notifications — is operated by Apple on your own device and under Apple's own terms, not ours.

10. Security

Because no data is transmitted, the usual risks of interception do not arise. On the device, the app relies on iOS file protection for its database, and on the iOS Keychain — device-only, available only while the phone is unlocked — for the app-lock PIN hash and recovery code. Please note that a four-digit PIN protects against someone picking up your unlocked phone; it is not designed to withstand forensic examination of the device itself.
